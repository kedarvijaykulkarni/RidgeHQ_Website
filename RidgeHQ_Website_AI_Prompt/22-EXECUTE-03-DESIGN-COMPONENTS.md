Design direction:

**Operational terrain / mission control for activity operators**.

Use dark indigo/slate base, restrained cyan/violet accents, real product UI, topographic/grid/path motifs, and selective glass treatment.

Do not make every card glow. Do not use generic robot art or a water-only/mountain-only identity.

Acceptance:

- WCAG-minded focus/contrast/touch targets;
- reduced motion;
- no oversized hero causing laptop overflow;
- components are reusable and typed;
- screenshots remain readable;
- minimal client JS.
