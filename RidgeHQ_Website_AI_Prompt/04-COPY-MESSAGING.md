# 04 — Copy and Messaging Specification

## Copy principle

Every section should answer:

> **What does this improve for the operator?**

Do not turn the website into a schema dump or a catalogue of nouns.

## Hero working direction

**Eyebrow:** `The Activity Business OS`

**Headline:** `Run your entire activity business from one HQ.`

**Subheadline direction:**

`RidgeHQ connects bookings, schedules, staff, customers, gear, rentals, trips, payments and daily operations in one live system — with an AI copilot built around the work.`

**Primary CTA:** `Book a Demo`

**Secondary CTA:** `Explore the Platform` or `Join the Design Partner Program`

Do not use `Start Free` while broad self-service signup remains disabled.

## Problem

Preferred headline:

**Your operation is connected. Your tools aren’t.**

Address real operational friction:

- schedule changes;
- instructor/guide availability;
- qualifications and language;
- customer/participant context;
- capacity;
- resource conflicts;
- rentals and maintenance;
- deposits/payments;
- weather/conditions;
- front-desk sales;
- partner attribution/commission;
- reporting and audit history;
- fragmented messaging/spreadsheets.

Never insult operators for using spreadsheets or WhatsApp. Position fragmentation as a scale/coordination limit, not incompetence.

## Solution

Headline direction:

**One system for the entire operational day.**

Explain shared state across:

- online booking;
- front desk/POS;
- Event Planner;
- staff;
- participants/clients;
- gear/rentals;
- trips/fleet;
- accommodation;
- payments;
- partners;
- reporting/audit.

## Benefit language

### Plan with the full picture
See sessions, people, capacity and resources together.

### Sell without creating a second operation
Keep front-desk and online sales connected to the same operational context.

### Protect scarce resources
Know what staff, gear, rooms, boats, bikes, or other units are already committed.

### Move when the day changes
Coordinate rescheduling and its operational impact instead of rebuilding the plan manually.

### Know the participant behind the booking
Keep customer and participant context attached to the activity workflow.

### Close the day with context
Bring payments, operational history and reporting together.

## AI Copilot

Preferred headline:

**AI that works inside the operation.**

Safe story, after code verification:

- understands current operational context;
- can inspect bookings/sessions/staff context;
- can use controlled product tools;
- medium/high-risk actions can require confirmation;
- non-read AI actions are auditable;
- selected actions have explicit undo support;
- provider choice can include Anthropic, OpenAI, and Ollama if still current.

Do not say:

- fully autonomous;
- replaces your team;
- every action is reversible;
- auto-refunds/cancels without operator control;
- automatic provider failover;
- “zero-error scheduling.”

## Multi-vertical message

Preferred headline:

**One operational model. Different ways to run the day.**

Support:

`Whether you schedule instructors, boats, boards, bikes, rooms or guided trips, the core challenge is similar: coordinate people, capacity, resources, time and payment without losing the operational picture.`

This expresses architectural breadth without claiming validated adoption across every vertical.

## Commercial/pricing message

Default public language:

- predictable subscription model;
- 0% RidgeHQ platform commission on direct bookings, if still current;
- pilot/early-access conversation;
- no free plan promise;
- no final numeric tier ladder unless explicitly approved later.

Do **not** publish the private €49/month 90-day pilot as a permanent public price merely because it exists in the business plan.

## Pilot/design-partner message

Use language like:

- Help shape the operational workflows that matter most.
- Use RidgeHQ with real operations and give structured feedback.
- Early access is selective so founder onboarding remains hands-on.
- We are validating fit and packaging with real operators before broad self-service launch.

Do not create fake scarcity countdowns or claim limited spots unless the founder has actually set a cap.

## Forms copy

The same Zoho form will appear in different contexts. Page copy should explain why the visitor is being asked for information.

Preferred labels:

- `How can we help?`
- `Work email`
- `Business name`
- `Business type`
- `What are you using today?`
- `What is the biggest operational problem you want to solve?`

Avoid asking for sensitive participant, payment-card, health, certification-document, or identity data on a marketing lead form.

## Social proof rule

Until proof exists, use:

- product screenshots;
- architecture/process proof;
- verified capabilities;
- operator-informed design language;
- transparent early-access positioning.

Do not manufacture logos, star ratings, booking counts, testimonials, ARR, time-saved percentages, or customer outcomes.
