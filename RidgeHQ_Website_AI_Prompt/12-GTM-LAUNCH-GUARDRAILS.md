# 12 — Website GTM and Launch Guardrails

## Why this file exists

The website is not merely a design artifact. It is part of a commercial experiment governed by the current `launch-plan.md`, `pricing.md`, and `unit-economics.md`.

The implementation must support the experiment instead of silently changing it.

## Current decision

**Controlled paid pilot now; broad self-service launch later.**

Website conversion priorities:

1. serious operator conversations;
2. demos;
3. design-partner / early-access applications;
4. pilot interest;
5. measurable source/vertical attribution.

Do not optimize for free account registrations because that is not the current commercial motion.

## Current validation scoreboard

The Brain snapshot records these business gates:

- first paid customer target by 2026-09-25;
- 3 paying customers by 2026-10-15;
- 5 paying customers by 2026-11-11;
- 90-day decision based on usage, retention, support burden and willingness to pay.

These are internal validation gates, not promises to publish on the website.

## Pricing state

Private pilot model:

- €49/month for 90 days;
- 0% RidgeHQ platform commission on direct bookings;
- founder onboarding;
- recurring feedback expected;
- not lifetime pricing.

Public website default:

- do not print the private pilot price as permanent public packaging;
- do not print €49/€89/€149 as validated self-service tiers;
- explain predictable subscription + 0% direct-booking platform commission;
- route interested operators into the Zoho lead flow.

## Design partner state

Do not promise “free forever.”

The Brain allows at most 1–2 strategically valuable free design partners, with structured feedback. Everyone else can be evaluated for the paid pilot.

The website should collect enough information to distinguish:

- serious operational tester;
- demo prospect;
- generic curiosity;
- partner/integration enquiry.

## Multi-vertical state

The website is broad because the master product architecture is broad.

The early commercial ICP remains narrower.

Therefore the CRM should capture vertical and operation-size context so GTM can prioritize without turning the public website into a dive-only brand.

## No paid-growth assumption

Do not add advertising-specific landing-page clutter or scripts merely because they may be useful later.

Support UTM/GCLID capture so paid acquisition can be turned on later, but current `launch-plan.md` says paid ads wait until demo-to-paid conversion and CAC evidence exist.

## Metrics to make possible

The website/CRM setup should enable later measurement of:

- lead volume by source;
- lead volume by vertical;
- demo/design-partner/pilot intent;
- demo-to-qualified conversion;
- qualified-to-pilot conversion;
- pilot-to-paid conversion;
- time to first response;
- eventual CAC by channel;
- retention/source quality after enough customers exist.

Do not fabricate these numbers before data exists.
