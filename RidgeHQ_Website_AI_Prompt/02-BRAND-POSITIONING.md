# 02 — RidgeHQ Brand, Positioning and GTM Boundary

## Brand role

RidgeHQ is the **master brand** for an operations platform serving businesses that schedule activities, lessons, trips, rentals, camps, or resort operations.

Interpret the name as:

> **The operational HQ for an activity business.**

Do not make “Ridge” mountain-only. The visual and verbal system must work across water, snow, land, tours, rentals, camps, and resorts.

## Primary category

Preferred:

**Activity Business OS**

Supporting language:

- Activity Operations Platform
- Operations software for activity businesses
- Booking + operations platform
- One operating system for lessons, trips, rentals and activity centers

Avoid defining RidgeHQ as:

- sports team management;
- dive-only software at master-brand level;
- generic rostering;
- merely a booking widget;
- merely an AI chatbot.

## Current commercial phase

RidgeHQ is not being marketed as a mature mass-market SaaS with settled packaging.

The website supports:

- operator discovery;
- demo requests;
- design-partner applications;
- Founding Operator Pilot interest;
- category education;
- product proof;
- qualified inbound lead capture.

Primary CTA default: **Book a Demo**

Secondary conversion CTA: **Join the Design Partner Program** / **Request Early Access**

Do not add **Start Free**, **Create Account**, or self-service checkout as the default conversion path while `launch-plan.md` says public self-service onboarding is disabled.

## GTM boundary

Architecture and website breadth do not equal equal market validation.

Current Phase-1 business strategy prioritizes dive centers, dive resorts, and closely related freediving/snorkel operators for early discovery and pilot recruitment.

Therefore:

- homepage/platform story remains broad;
- all 12 solution pages can exist for category/SEO/product architecture;
- CRM lead scoring may prioritize the current beachhead;
- do not claim RidgeHQ already serves all 12 verticals.

## Brand promise

**Know what is booked, assigned, available and paid — from one HQ.**

## Differentiation hierarchy

### 1. Operations after the booking

The booking is the starting point. RidgeHQ connects it to sessions, participants, staff, resources, payments, changes, and reporting.

### 2. Activity-aware resource coordination

The operational day combines time, capacity, staff capability, locations, equipment, fleet, accommodation, and changing conditions.

### 3. AI inside the operational model

The Copilot is not a decorative chat bubble. Where verified, it uses the same product-service layer, permissions, risk confirmation, and audit model as human-initiated operations.

### 4. Shared operational state

Online booking, front desk, schedule, staff, inventory, partners, and reporting should not behave like separate businesses.

### 5. Predictable commercial model

0% RidgeHQ platform commission on direct bookings is a supporting trust message while current, not the only reason to buy.

## Voice

RidgeHQ sounds:

- capable;
- practical;
- calm;
- modern;
- operator-first;
- precise;
- international.

Avoid:

- hype-heavy AI language;
- consumer extreme-sports branding;
- luxury-only resort language;
- “revolutionary”, “disruptive”, “world-class AI”;
- “trusted by thousands” or “#1” without proof.
