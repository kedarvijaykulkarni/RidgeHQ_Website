_A surf camp guest books a week that includes lessons, board hire and a bed. Each of those comes with risk, and the paperwork has to follow the guest, not the booking form._

## A Camp Week Is Several Products

Camp packages bundle accommodation and gear, often with add-ons such as transfers. Lessons, rentals and rooms sit on one calendar. That is exactly where loose paperwork goes wrong: a waiver signed on arrival, filed by hand, against a booking someone later changes.

## Waivers Are Part of the Booking

In RidgeHQ, waivers are built in rather than sold as a separate add-on. Waiver requirements attach to products, and every participant on a booking completes the waiver with a typed e-signature before checkout can be submitted. A group of four produces four signed records.

Each signature is stored with the exact text the guest agreed to, their answers, a content hash and the signing device. If you later edit the template, earlier signatures still show what that guest signed. Waiver records are protected by database-level row-level security like the rest of your data.

## Questions That Fit the Guest

Waiver templates support conditional questions: a follow-up appears only when an earlier answer calls for it, for example a swimming-confidence question for someone who has never surfed. A health questionnaire needs separate health-data consent, is visible only to senior roles, and every read is audited.

## Returning Guests and Renewals

A template can carry a validity window, so a signature stays current for a set number of months, then shows as expiring or expired. When a guest needs to sign or renew outside checkout, staff can capture it at the desk or email a single-use renewal link that expires after seven days.

## On the Day

Staff check guests in from the session roster, and guests can check in everyone on their confirmed booking by scanning a per-booking QR code. QR-linked waiver *signing* is not built yet and is on the roadmap; today guests sign at checkout, by renewal link, or with staff at the desk.

## Key takeaways

- Waivers are built in and enforced per participant with a typed e-signature before checkout.
- Conditional questions, validity windows and renewal links suit multi-day camps and returning guests.
- Health answers need separate consent, are restricted to senior roles, and every read is audited.
- Staff check-in and guest QR check-in are live; QR-linked signing is planned.
