_A windsurf school's revenue follows the wind: a strong week fills every lesson and rig, a flat week fills almost nothing. Software that charges per booking moves with that curve. A flat monthly subscription does not._

## Why Per-Booking Fees Are Hard to Plan Around

Windsurf, wingfoil and SUP schools earn most of their year in a short season, and even inside it demand swings with the forecast. When software is paid as a percentage of each booking, its cost rises on exactly the busy days and is impossible to fix in a budget ahead of time.

RidgeHQ is sold as a flat monthly subscription with no per-booking platform fee. The software line in your budget is the same in a windy August as in a quiet May, so you can plan the season around instructors, boards and rigs rather than around fees.

## What the Subscription Covers on a Windsurf Floor

The subscription covers the operating system for the school, not a booking page bolted onto spreadsheets. Lessons, courses and rentals sit in one schedule (the Event Planner), with instructor assignments and wind, tide and weather suitability for the spot. Boards, rigs and wetsuits are tracked as rental gear, and a unit on a maintenance hold drops out of availability until it is back. The booking widget, the front-desk POS and partner bookings share one order model, and paid orders are never edited in place: a change is recorded as a credit note.

Staff work inside seven fixed, server-enforced roles, and the AI Copilot helps with reading and scheduling within those roles.

## Keeping the Customer Relationship

Predictable cost is half of it. The other half is who owns the customer. When a student books through your own site using the RidgeHQ booking widget, the booking, the customer record and the payment are yours. Card payments go to your school's own Stripe account, and RidgeHQ takes 0% platform commission on those direct bookings on every plan. Stripe's own processing fees still apply.

That customer record carries the student's bookings, waivers and history, so a returning student is someone you already know. A manage-booking link lets customers cancel or reschedule under your school's policy; refunds are not automatic and stay your decision.

If you work with hotels, camps or agents, their bookings are recorded as partner-attributed orders and follow that partner's commission rule, so settlement stays clear without changing the 0% on your direct bookings.
