_When a student pays for a sailing course, the money should land in the school's account, not sit with a booking platform that pays it out later._

## Where the Money Goes Matters

A sailing school takes payments for courses, private lessons and boat time, often months ahead of the season. If those payments are collected by a booking platform on the school's behalf, the platform sits between the customer and the school's account, and it can be hard to see at a glance what has actually been received.

Financial visibility starts with a simple question: whose account did this payment go into? For a school running on thin seasonal margins, the answer should be "ours".

## Direct Charges on Your Own Stripe Account

In RidgeHQ, card payments are direct charges on the school's own Stripe account. The payment is made to the school, not collected by RidgeHQ and passed on.

To take card payments, the school needs an active Stripe Connect account linked to RidgeHQ. Stripe is the payment gateway, and Stripe's own processing fees apply as they would for any Stripe account. Gift vouchers also need Stripe connected.

Because payments are recorded against the order they settle, a payment in Stripe and a booking in RidgeHQ refer to the same order rather than living in two unconnected systems.

## No Platform Commission on Direct Bookings

RidgeHQ takes 0% platform commission on direct bookings, on every plan. A student who books a course through the school's own booking widget does not trigger a percentage cut to RidgeHQ.

Bookings that come through a partner, such as a hotel or agent, follow that partner's commission rule, and the commission is recorded with the order for settlement. That keeps partner costs visible as partner costs, separate from direct revenue. The same model is covered for dive centres in [flat pricing and 0% commission on direct bookings](/blog/flat-pricing-0-commission-direct-bookings-dive-centers).

## Records That Stay Trustworthy

Visibility is not only about where the money goes, but about whether the record of it holds up later.

- **Paid orders are not edited in place.** A change to a paid order is recorded as a credit note, so the original payment and the adjustment both remain on record. See [order immutability and credit notes](/blog/order-immutability-credit-note-on-change).
- **Changes are logged.** The audit log records who made a change.
- **Revenue visibility is controlled by role.** Revenue reporting is limited to the roles that need it, so instructors can work with bookings without seeing the school's takings.
